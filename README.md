# Bryan Hurst: portfolio site

Plain HTML and CSS. No build step and nothing to install.

## Files
- `index.html`: home page (intro, career path, projects, about, contact)
- `vantage.html`: Vantage case study
- `styles.css`: all the styling. Colors and fonts are at the top under "Design tokens"
- `images/`: compressed screenshots (WebP)

## Preview on your computer
Double-click `index.html` to open it in your browser.

## Put it online with GitHub Pages (free)
1. Create a new public repo on GitHub, for example `portfolio`.
2. Upload these files (keep the `images` folder).
3. In the repo, go to Settings > Pages. Under "Branch", pick `main` and `/ (root)`, then Save.
4. After a minute or two, your site is live at `https://senpai-bry.github.io/portfolio/`.

To use your own domain later, add it under Settings > Pages > Custom domain and point the DNS at GitHub, the same way you pointed vantageturf.com at Railway.
